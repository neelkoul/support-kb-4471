Each row is one ticket. The counts are computed from the export at render time (src/lib/kb/problems.ts). The export repeats some reports, so they are counted once by id.

How they differ

  • Parser. The shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants write the day first, so 7 April comes out as 4 July. This is the problem #4471 describes.
  • Form hint. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what it says, and the form reads the date day first. The date is already flipped before it reaches the parser. Only reports that came in through a booking-v3 form belong here.
  • One day off. In these reports the day and month are not swapped. The booking moves by a day, or differs between people in different time zones. The desk already listed these in desk-notes.json as a one-day move, but the #4471 table counts them under the parser.

How to place a report

Do not trust intakeTag. Intake guesses it from the reporter’s browser. The cause depends on the tenant’s locale, its booking form and the channel (causeOf in src/lib/kb/causes.ts). Then read the body: a swap of day and month is the parser or the form hint, and a one-day move is its own problem.

For the desk

  • Keep #4471 for the parser problem only. Move the other reports to their own tickets.
  • A shared subject line is not the same cause. Decision 0001 needs a successor record.
